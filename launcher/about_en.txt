= About
[hero about_shot_cb.jpg|logo_cb.png|30|]
Clusterball came out in 2000 and still has a loyal community. On today's computers, though, it barely runs: Glide and the old OpenGL drivers are gone, its resolutions date from the days of CRT monitors, and the old launcher depended on services that were switched off long ago.
Re-Enhanced makes the original playable again without changing it:
- A Direct3D 11 renderer of its own instead of Glide and OpenGL, at any resolution up to 4K, with anti-aliasing and ambient occlusion.
- HD textures (4x), switchable off.
- Sharp text in menus and HUD, mouse wheel and WASD controls.
- This launcher: servers, settings and manual in one place.
Gameplay, flight model, weapons and worlds stay exactly as they were. It is the same Clusterball, just running again – so the original can be played alongside its successor. Accounts, ranking and forum are provided by the community at clusterball.de.

ClusterForce is a spiritual successor to Clusterball, made by Team ClusterForce: a fast arcade 3D flying sports game for up to 8 players online.
- Pilot one of 8 electromagnetic flyers, the gliders, across the sports grounds, the venue
- Collect the energy balls from the magnetic ramps
- Use the weapons scattered across the map to disrupt your rivals or steal their balls
- Score by delivering as many balls as you can to the goal zone
